# Collection and record cover images

Please supply one **3:2 landscape cover image at 1500 × 1000 pixels** for each collection and record. Use JPEG or WebP in sRGB. A larger image with the same ratio, such as 2400 × 1600 pixels, is also suitable.

- Fill the entire image with the photograph or a deliberate photographic background. Do not add black bars, borders, text, or a logo.
- Keep the full specimen or important geological feature inside the frame, with breathing room around it. For a tall specimen, compose it on a wider background rather than cutting off its top or bottom.
- Keep important details away from corners because the website places record labels there.
- Supply an image description, caption, and photographer/source credit separately.
- Also supply the original, uncropped photograph for detailed examination. Additional gallery images can keep their original proportions.

The Explore collection cards and record cards both display covers at 3:2. Matching covers fill the frame without added bars or aspect-ratio cropping. Existing images with other proportions are center-cropped in card previews, without stretching or hover zoom. The record gallery and enlarged viewer continue to show the complete original image.

This is a showcase presentation guideline, not an upload restriction. Hero images and other pages are outside this specification.
